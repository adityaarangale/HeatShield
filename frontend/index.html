Citizen Heat Safety Advisory

🚰 Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly, even if you don't feel thirsty. Avoid caffeinated and alcoholic beverages as they can dehydrate you.

☀️ Avoid Peak Heat

Stay indoors during the hottest hours (11 AM to 4 PM). If you must go outside, seek shade and wear light, loose-fitting clothing.

👵 Check Vulnerable People

Regularly check on elderly family members, children, and those with chronic illnesses. Ensure they have access to cool spaces and water.

❄️ Use Cooling Spaces

Visit air-conditioned public spaces like malls, libraries, or cooling centers during extreme heat. Use fans and take cool showers.

👷 Outdoor Worker Safety

Take frequent breaks in shaded areas. Wear protective clothing and stay hydrated. Know the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke.
