RISK DRIVER BREAKDOWN

Underlying Formulas & Calculation Methodology:

• NWS Heat Index: Rothfusz 9-parameter regression equation using C-to-F ambient temperature & relative humidity.

• Australian BOM WBGT: 0.567 * T + 0.393 * e + 3.94 where vapor pressure e = (RH/100) * 6.105 * exp(17.27*T / (237.7+T)).

• Blended HTSI (60% Weight): 0.5 * SolarAdjustedWBGT + 0.3 * ApparentTemp + 0.2 * HeatIndex normalized linearly over range [25°C, 50°C].

• Demographic Component (40% Weight): 0.4 * ElderlyPct + 0.4 * OutdoorWorkersPct + 0.2 * (100 - GreenCoverPct).
